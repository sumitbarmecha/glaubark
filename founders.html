The Founding Story

A research paper.
A personal calling.
A shared vision.

Glaubark Solutions was born at the intersection of two worlds — the rigor of climate finance research and the lived reality of agrarian distress. What began as an internship insight grew into a company working across Maharashtra, Punjab, Kerala, and Meghalaya, approaching 100,000 hectares of land under carbon project development.

Behind it are two co-founders who brought different perspectives, backgrounds, and strengths — united by a single conviction that climate action and farmer welfare are the same goal.

01 Co-founder
Marathwada, Maharashtra

Dhruv Sakore

Co-founder & CEO

Dhruv grew up in Marathwada — one of Maharashtra's most drought-prone regions, where unseasonal rainfall, depleted soils, and volatile crop prices erode farmer livelihoods over generations. Agriculture wasn't an abstract subject for him; it was home.

While interning at Tata Sons in Mumbai, he was writing a research paper on green finance and the global energy transition. In that research, he discovered carbon credits — and immediately saw their potential not just as an environmental accounting mechanism, but as a direct financing tool for farmers.

That realisation became Glaubark. If farmers adopted regenerative practices that improved soil health, sequestered carbon, and reduced emissions, they should also earn from the carbon credit market. Climate action and farmer welfare didn't have to be separate goals.

"Carbon credits should not just be traded in boardrooms. They should reach the hands that grow our food."

— Dhruv Sakore

02 Co-founder

Vatsal Agarwal

Co-founder & COO

Vatsal was in London, studying and looking for an opportunity to build something meaningful — not just something that would work on paper, but something that would create tangible change at scale.

When Dhruv shared the Glaubark idea with him, Vatsal saw it clearly: a genuine market gap, a real social need, and a founder with deep personal skin in the game. After several intense conversations about the model, the mission, and the path forward, he decided to come on board.

Vatsal brings a systems-level perspective to Glaubark's operations — from building the structures that make carbon project development repeatable, to forging the institutional partnerships that connect Indian farmland to global climate finance.

Shared Principles

What both founders believe

Farmers First

Farmers are participants in the process, not just beneficiaries. Every decision is made with their welfare as the primary measure of success.

Ground-Up Verification

Real on-farm data, not assumptions. MRV infrastructure built from the ground level up to meet international carbon standards.

Skin in the Game

The founders spend real time in the field. Decisions are shaped by what works on-farm, not what looks good on a slide deck.

Speed & Rigour Together

Moving fast enough to matter for farmers facing climate stress, while building the documentation quality that credit buyers demand.

Climate & Livelihoods

Proving that healing the planet and improving farmer incomes are not competing goals — they are the same goal pursued together.

Regenerative by Design

Every intervention must improve soil, water, and biodiversity — carbon sequestration is the outcome, not a bolt-on metric.
