01 — Positioning

Climate finance that starts in the field.

Farmer-first carbon project developer. Not an NGO, charity, SaaS product, marketplace, or crypto protocol.

Always show two or more

Farmers · regenerative agriculture · climate science · carbon finance · technology · measurement.

Feel

Credible, technical, human, premium, practical, evidence-led.

Never feel

Generic “green,” CSR charity, tractor catalogue, dashboard startup, Web3.

Personality

Grounded · Evidence-led · Farmer-first · Technical not cold · Considered · Practical.

Story (keep, don’t embroider)

Tata Sons research → Marathwada roots → credits as farmer finance → two founders → field projects in India. No invented quotes or titles.

02 — Voice

Calm, specific, field-literate.

Sentence case. No exclamation marks. Farmers are partners, not beneficiaries.

Do say

  • Farmer-first carbon project developer
  • Regenerative practices on enrolled farms
  • MRV, baseline, verification support
  • District, crop, field team
  • Climate finance as rural income

Avoid

  • “Offsetting carbon for future generations”
  • VCS certified / registry-listed (unless confirmed)
  • 2,800% · 88% · agentic AI · fake farmers
  • “Transform farmland into a carbon asset”
  • Saving the planet, tokens, beneficiaries

Short: Farmer-first carbon projects: regenerative agriculture, measurement, and climate finance in India.

Forest is the room. Lime is the signal.

Cream/white majority. Forest 25–35% on dark pages. Leaf on light. Lime ≤5%.

04 — Type

DM Sans headlines. Inter everything else.

Load both on every page. Headlines 600 / −0.01em / lh 1.2. Sentence case. Lowercase glaubark on loader and footer mark.

05 — Logo

White PNG wordmark. Don’t redraw it.

Heights

Header 28 / 60px. Footer 30px. Loader mark 68–80px. Never below 28px digital.

On dark / light

Screen blend on forest. Invert + multiply on cream. Prefer a true ink master once supplied.

Don’t

Recolour, stretch, drop shadow, replace with unused logo.svg, busy crop with no veil.

06 — Imagery

Documentary Indian agriculture.

Use

  • Real farmers and field teams (permissioned)
  • Soil, crop, drip, residue, devices in the field
  • Natural light · assets/images/shoot/
  • Hero video: full-bleed, ~30% black veil

Don’t

  • Wind turbines, ice, globes, green gradients
  • Handshakes, cartoon CO₂, crypto seals
  • Staged smiling-farmer stock, Unsplash “founders”
  • Stroke icons only — no filled eco packs

08 — Trust

If it isn’t confirmed, omit it.

The live site still contains placeholders. They are not brand.

Never publish

2,800% · 88.8% · SaaS leftover % · fictional farmers · “VCS certified” as a badge · invented SLAs.

Confirm first

Hectares, titles (CEO/COO vs Founder), emails, social URLs, partner logos, quotes.

Safe positioning

Farmer-first project developer. Maharashtra districts. Practice menu. MRV as field tools. Origin sequence.

09 — Before you publish

Sixty-second checklist

  • Approved wordmark, not retyped
  • Palette from tokens — no extra emerald
  • Inter + DM Sans
  • 1400px and 20/32/48 gutters
  • Shoot photography or approved stills
  • One sentence-case CTA
  • No unsourced number
  • No invented names or testimonials
  • Carbon-standard language matches status
  • Farmers as partners
  • Origin story not exaggerated
  • Draft pages not quoted as live proof
