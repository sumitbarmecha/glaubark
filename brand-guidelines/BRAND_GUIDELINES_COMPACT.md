# Glaubark — Compact brand guide

Desk reference. For full rationale, open `BRAND_GUIDELINES.md` or `BRAND_GUIDELINES.html`.

**Essence:** Climate finance that starts in the field.  
**Category:** Farmer-first carbon project developer. Not an NGO, SaaS product, marketplace, or crypto protocol.

Always show at least two of: farmers · regenerative agriculture · climate science · carbon finance · technology · measurement.

---

## Personality

Grounded · Evidence-led · Farmer-first · Technical not cold · Considered · Practical.

## Voice

Calm, specific, field-literate. Sentence case. Farmers are partners, not beneficiaries.

| Do say | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Farmer-first carbon project developer | Offsetting carbon for future generations |
| Regenerative practices on enrolled farms | VCS certified / registry-listed (unless confirmed) |
| MRV, baseline, verification support | 2,800% · 88% · agentic AI · fictional farmers |
| District, crop, field team | Transform farmland into a carbon asset |

**Short description:** Farmer-first carbon projects: regenerative agriculture, measurement, and climate finance in India.

**Story (keep, don’t embroider):** Tata Sons research → Marathwada → credits as farmer finance → two founders → field projects in India.

---

## Colour

Forest is the room. Lime is the signal. Leaf is the only green on cream. Lime ≤5%.

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Forest | `#0d1a10` | Dark band, footer, loader |
| Forest Deep | `#082410` | Deepest heroes |
| Header | `#132517` | Scrolled header, dark panels |
| Leaf | `#22804a` | Links, icons on cream |
| Lime | `#a3e635` | Accent on dark |
| Cream | `#f7f5f0` | Light wash |
| Ink / Sub / Line | `#1c1c1c` / `#6b6b6b` / `#e8e4db` | Text, captions, borders |
| Sage | `#9fd7a4` | Whisper only |

Do not use Sage or Lime as body text on cream. Do not add `#2fce65` or emerald. Accent hex `#61af1e` does not match its RGB token.

---

## Type

**DM Sans** 600 for headings (−0.01em, lh 1.2). **Inter** 400–600 for body and UI. Load both on every page.  
Eyebrow: 11px / 0.15em / uppercase. Buttons: 14px / 600 / pill, sentence case.  
Lowercase **glaubark** on loader and giant footer mark.

---

## Logo

Primary: `assets/images/glaubark_logo.png` (white PNG). Mark: `logo_icon.png`. Do not use unused `logo.svg`.  
Heights: header 28 / 60px · footer 30px · never below 28px.  
Dark: screen blend. Light: invert + multiply. No recolour, stretch, or drop shadow.

---

## Imagery

Documentary Indian agriculture from `assets/images/shoot/`. Natural light, field work, tech in someone’s hands.  
Hero video: full-bleed + ~30% black veil. Stroke SVG icons.  
Never: wind turbines, globes, green gradients, handshake stock, fake founders.

---

## Layout / UI

1400px · gutters 20 / 32 / 48 · header 65/70px · nav hamburger &lt; 1024px.  
Radius: pills 9999px, cards 1.25–2.25rem. Space scale 4–128. Sections 64–128px.  
Lime pill on dark; Leaf on cream. One primary CTA. Cinematic motion; honour `prefers-reduced-motion`.

---

## Claims

If it isn’t confirmed, omit it. Never publish live-site leftovers as brand: **2,800%**, **88.8%**, fictional farmers, “VCS certified” as a present-tense badge, invented SLAs.

Confirm before use: hectares, founder titles, emails, social URLs, quotes, partner logos.
