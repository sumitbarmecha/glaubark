## How to use this book

A designer, writer, or developer should be able to produce a Glaubark page, deck, case study, or LinkedIn post from these files without a verbal briefing.

Throughout, every material statement is tagged:

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Observed** | Directly found in the current website or repository |
| **Brand Rule** | A rule that can reasonably be formalised from that implementation |
| **Strategic Recommendation** | Guidance for future use that was not explicit in the site |
| **Client Confirmation Required** | Needs Glaubark’s approval or factual verification |

The website is the primary source of truth for **look**. The client brief and `CLIENT-REVIEW-ASSUMED-CONTENT.md` are the source of truth for **facts**. Where the live site still shows invented statistics, fictional farmers, or over-strong certification language, this book **does not** promote those as brand.

## 1. Brand essence

**Observed positioning (site + brief, strategic articulation — not a legal descriptor):**

Glaubark develops farmer-first carbon projects at the intersection of regenerative agriculture, climate science, carbon finance, technology, and measurement.

**Essence (Strategic Recommendation — distilled, not a client-supplied slogan):**

> Climate finance that starts in the field.

**Promise (Strategic Recommendation, aligned to About mission without inflating issuance status):**

Farmers who adopt regenerative practices should be able to improve their land and participate in voluntary carbon markets through a project that is measured, documented, and taken seriously by counterparties.

**It is not:** an NGO, a charity, a generic agribusiness, a carbon marketplace, a registry, a SaaS dashboard company, or a Web3 protocol.

## 2. Positioning

### 2.1 The intersection (Brand Rule)

Every piece of communication should make at least two of these legs visible. Never only “green”. Never only “tech”.

```
FARMERS
+ REGENERATIVE AGRICULTURE
+ CLIMATE SCIENCE
+ CARBON FINANCE
+ TECHNOLOGY
+ MEASUREMENT
```

### 2.2 Category

**Observed:** “farmer-first carbon project developer”; “bridging regenerative farming and voluntary carbon markets”; regenerative farming and carbon credits for India’s farmers (footer tagline).

**Brand Rule:** Lead with **project development + farmers**, not with offsets as a lifestyle product.

The current home H1 — “Offsetting carbon footprint for future generations” — is **Observed** and also flagged in the proofing log as generic climate-template language. **Strategic Recommendation:** do not reuse it on new materials. Prefer lines that name farmers, practices, or project development.

### 2.3 Competitive / category “nots” (Strategic Recommendation)

| Do not resemble | Why it fails Glaubark |
|---|---|
| Generic environmental NGO | Charity palette, leaf logos, donation CTAs |
| Generic agriculture company | Tractor catalogues, commodity crop ads |
| Generic SaaS | Gradient blobs, dashboard-first heroes, “agentic AI” |
| Crypto / Web3 carbon | Tokens, chains, neon, speculative tone |
| Corporate consulting | Stock handshakes, navy-and-teal templates |
| Stereotypical “green” sustainability | Lime washes, wind turbines, globe icons, stock smiles |

## 3. Brand personality

Six traits. Each is visible on the implemented site; the “avoid” column stops the trait from decaying.

### Grounded

**Meaning:** Glaubark is from a real agrarian geography (Marathwada) and works in named districts, not “the Global South.”  
**Visual:** Documentary field photography; cream and forest rather than neon eco-green; type that sits still.  
**Verbal:** Place names, crop names, process steps.  
**Avoid:** Rustic pastiche, hand-drawn barns, faux-khadi clichés.

### Evidence-led

**Meaning:** Trust is built by showing the work (practices, MRV, sequence), not by shouting impact.  
**Visual:** Restrained stats (when real), process diagrams, readable captions.  
**Verbal:** Qualify claims; prefer “support” over “certified” until true.  
**Avoid:** Oversized fake percentages (the live hero 2,800% / 88.8% must not be treated as brand).

### Farmer-first

**Meaning:** Farmers are counterparties in a project, not beneficiaries of a campaign.  
**Visual:** People working, talking, measuring — not posed charity portraits.  
**Verbal:** Enrolment, field teams, income as one outcome among soil, water, and market access.  
**Avoid:** Saviour narratives; “giving voice to.”

### Technical, not cold

**Meaning:** MRV, GIS, baselines, and standards are part of the identity — always attached to a field.  
**Visual:** A phone or map in a farm, not a dark-mode analytics product shot.  
**Verbal:** Spell out MRV once; then use it. No fake product names.  
**Avoid:** Lab coats in glass towers; sci-fi HUD overlays.

### Considered / premium

**Meaning:** The site is editorial: 1400px, generous type, dark cinematic heroes, quiet cream.  
**Visual:** Space, rounded panels, few colours at once, lime as a signal not a fill.  
**Verbal:** Short headlines; no exclamation marks.  
**Avoid:** Luxury fashion tone; gold foil; “exclusive.”

### Practical

**Meaning:** Glaubark sells a working cycle, not a dream.  
**Visual:** Practice tags (soil health, water efficiency, field-verified MRV) rather than abstract icons.  
**Verbal:** What happens next. Who visits. What is measured.  
**Avoid:** Manifesto copy with no mechanism.

## 4. Brand principles (working values)

Themes **Observed** on About / brief (wording is ours where the site paraphrases): Farmer First, climate integrity, ground-level execution, livelihoods, transparency, innovation, local roots / global impact.

**Strategic Recommendation — use these as decision filters, not poster slogans:**

1. **The field is the product surface.** If a layout could be swapped onto a fintech and still work, it is wrong.
2. **Integrity before volume.** Do not imply issued, listed, or certified credits unless that is the current status.
3. **Name the place.** Maharashtra, Marathwada, a district, a crop — specificity is the brand.
4. **Technology explains; it does not decorate.**
5. **Long-term relationships, not campaign drops.** Photography and copy should look like return visits, not a one-day CSR shoot.

## 5. Brand story

### 5.1 Structure to keep (Observed on About)

Tell it as a sequence, not a myth.

1. **Bombay House / Tata Sons, Mumbai.** Dhruv Sakore, during an internship, researching green finance, encounters carbon credits as a way to fund climate-positive projects.
2. **Marathwada.** The idea lands personally: drought-prone region, agricultural family, farmer distress as lived knowledge.
3. **Reframe.** Carbon credits as a **financing tool for farmers** who regenerate land — climate action and farmer welfare as one project, not two causes.
4. **London.** The idea is shared; Vatsal Agarwal joins from studies in London; they found Glaubark Solutions.
5. **Today.** Work with farmers, FPOs, sugar factories, and agricultural partners. Maharashtra plus partner geographies named in the brief (Punjab, Kerala, Meghalaya). Scale line on site: approaching 100,000 hectares — **Client Confirmation Required** as a current figure.

### 5.2 Emotional role

The story earns the right to talk about carbon markets without sounding imported. It answers “why you?” with geography and research, not with a startup origin cliché.

### 5.3 Strategic role

It positions Glaubark as **Indian, agrarian, and financially literate** — capable of speaking to farmers and to buyers.

### 5.4 What must stay consistent

- Marathwada as root, not as a branding sticker
- Carbon credits discovered through green-finance research, not “we always knew”
- Two founders; farmer-first company
- No third fictional co-founder, no invented mill partnership in the origin

### 5.5 What must not be exaggerated

- Do not invent quotes (**Observed** quotes on founder cards were flagged as invented in the proofing log unless later confirmed).
- Titles: brief listed both as **Founder**. Site uses Co-founder & CEO / COO — **Client Confirmation Required**.
- Credentials (Columbia, LSE, EY, papers, Mission Prana): on the brief, thin or missing on the live site. Add only if the client wants them public.
- “Active across 4+ Indian states” is a count of named geographies, not automatically four operating offices.
- Do not add Karnataka (proofing log: not in the brief).

## 6. Messaging framework

### 6.1 Elevator pitch (Strategic Recommendation)

Glaubark is a farmer-first carbon project developer. We work with farmers and agricultural partners in India to adopt regenerative practices, measure what changes, and connect that work to voluntary carbon markets — so climate finance can become a rural income stream, not only a corporate offset story.

### 6.2 Short description (~15 words)

Farmer-first carbon projects: regenerative agriculture, measurement, and climate finance in India.

### 6.3 Medium description (~40 words)

Glaubark Solutions develops carbon projects with farmers, FPOs, and agricultural partners. Regenerative practices, field relationships, and MRV sit at the centre of the work — so soil, water, and livelihoods improve while projects are built to recognised carbon-market standards.

*(Last clause: **Client Confirmation Required** for how strongly to claim “recognised standards” vs “VCS project support.”)*

### 6.4 Long description

Use the About narrative + services cycle. Do not pad with unsourced impact metrics.

### 6.5 Messaging pillars (Brand Rule)

| Pillar | Proof on site (qualitative) | Do not fake |
|---|---|---|
| **Farmers** | Enrolment, field teams, district presence | Headcount, rupee uplift |
| **Regeneration** | Practice list (drip, residue, tillage, inputs, rotation, intercropping, …) | “10+ interventions on every farm” unless confirmed |
| **Measurement** | MRV, satellite, GIS, surveys as **tool types** | Named software product, fake dashboards |
| **Carbon finance** | Voluntary market access as the end of a cycle | Issued volume, vintage, buyer names |
| **Duration** | Long-term field relationships as a value | “∞ partnerships” as a statistic |

### 6.6 Audience-specific leads

See `CONTENT_RULES.md` § Audience cues. Same substance; different first sentence.

### 6.7 Homepage messaging principle (Strategic Recommendation)

Hero: **who + mechanism + geography**, then practices. Stats only if approved. Trust row: process and presence, not invented certifications (“Field-to-Market” is an invented badge).

### 6.8 CTA philosophy

One primary action. Promise a conversation or a document. Do not promise credit payments, co-branding, or 24-hour replies unless confirmed.

## 8. Visual identity — colour

Full tables: `DESIGN_TOKENS.md`.

### 8.1 The system as it feels (Observed)

Glaubark is **dark forest + warm cream + a sharp lime signal**. It is not emerald-on-white NGO, and not black-and-electric-green crypto.

- **Forest** (`#0d1a10`, `#082410`, `#132517`) carries authority, cinema, night-after-field-day.
- **Cream** (`#f7f5f0`) carries paper, heat, daylight interiors.
- **Leaf** (`#22804a`) is the only green that behaves as green on cream — links, icons, success.
- **Lime** (`#a3e635`) is for dark surfaces: accent headlines, pills, key marks.
- **Ink / Sub** are near-neutral. Type is charcoal, not forest-coloured paragraphs.

### 8.2 Primary / secondary / accent / neutrals / functional

| Role | Colours |
|---|---|
| Primary dark | Forest, Forest Deep |
| Primary on light | Leaf |
| Secondary | Forest Mid, Sage, panel `#132517` |
| Accent | Lime `#a3e635`; token Accent `#61af1e` (see mismatch) |
| Neutrals | Cream, White, Ink, Sub, Line |
| Functional | Leaf (success), `#b42318` (error toast) |

### 8.3 Usage share (Brand Rule)

On a typical marketing spread: cream/white ~50–60%, forest ~25–35%, leaf ~8–12%, lime ≤5%, sage as whisper.

### 8.4 Known inconsistencies (Observed)

- `--accent` / `--lime-soft` hex vs RGB mismatch (`DESIGN_TOKENS.md`).
- About dark UI uses `#84cc16` instead of `--lime`.
- Nav hover `#2fce65` is outside the palette.
- Scrolled header `#132517` is not a CSS variable.

**Strategic Recommendation:** one header forest, one lime on dark, Leaf on light, no extra bright greens.

## 9. Visual identity — typography

### Pair

**Inter** (body, UI) + **DM Sans** (headings, loader word, giant footer mark).

This is the implemented pair. Do not “upgrade” to Space Grotesk, Manrope, or a serif headline for campaigns.

Personality: Inter is infrastructural and readable (climate-tech, forms, nav). DM Sans is slightly more geometric and editorial — enough distinction for headlines without looking like a fashion magazine or a playful startup.

**Observed gap:** DM Sans is loaded on Home only; CSS still declares it for all `h1–h6`. **Brand Rule:** load both fonts everywhere so the system is real, not accidental.

Occasional **Georgia** for quotes is a small editorial exception, not a third brand font. Do not expand serif use.

### Hierarchy

See `DESIGN_TOKENS.md` § Typography for sizes, tracking, and the uppercase eyebrow convention.

**Brand Rule:** Headlines sentence case; loader and footer giant mark **lowercase glaubark**; never faux small-caps.

## 10. Logo

### 10.1 What exists (Observed)

| Asset | Role |
|---|---|
| `assets/images/glaubark_logo.png` | Primary white wordmark |
| `assets/images/logo_icon.png` | Circular mark (loader) |
| `assets/images/logo.svg` | Unused concentric-arc drawing |

Live use is the **PNG wordmark**, blended:

- Dark bar: `mix-blend-mode: screen` (white lockup sits on forest)
- Light article heroes: `multiply` + `invert(1)` until the header scrolls to forest

There is no separate stacked lockup. Favicon is not implemented.

### 10.2 Clear space & minimum size

**Not specified in production files.** The following are **Strategic Recommendation**, to be finalised with Glaubark:

- Clear space: at least the height of the “g” counter on all four sides.
- Minimum digital wordmark: **28px** height (current mobile header — do not go smaller).
- Prefer **60px** height in primary headers at desktop, matching the live site.
- Loader mark: 68–80px is the current ceremonial size, not the UI minimum.

Do not invent a 2.5x-x-height rule and present it as existing.

### 10.3 Backgrounds (Brand Rule from implementation)

| Background | Wordmark treatment |
|---|---|
| Forest, Forest Deep, `#132517`, photography with dark veil | White PNG + screen blend (or plain white if blend is unavailable in print) |
| Cream, White, light photography | Dark wordmark — invert the PNG or, once supplied, use an ink master |
| Lime or Sage fills | Avoid; low dignity, poor contrast |

### 10.4 Incorrect use (Brand Rule)

- Recolour to gold, black-green, or rainbow
- Add drop shadows, slogans inside the PNG, or a leaf growing from a letter
- Replace the live lockup with `logo.svg` without a brand decision
- Place on busy crop photography without a veil or solid panel
- Stretch, outline, or set in another typeface
- Use the giant footer “glaubark” setting as a logo substitute in headers

### 10.5 Placement (Observed → Brand Rule)

Header: left, vertically centred in 65/70px bar. Footer: brand column, 30px height, tagline under. Loader: mark + lowercase word, centred on Forest.

## 11. Imagery

### 11.1 What the site is trying to be (Observed + photography brief)

The strongest frames in `assets/images/shoot/` are **documentary Indian agriculture**: working bodies, real light, soil, crop rows, field conversation, technology as a phone or notebook in someone’s hands.

Hero **video** is full-bleed, veiled, cinematic — field time, not a corporate montage.

### 11.2 Visual language

| Axis | Direction |
|---|---|
| Style | Documentary first, lightly editorial crop |
| Light | Available / natural; avoid studio beauty lighting |
| People | Farmers, field teams, women and men working; not handshake crops |
| Place | Indian agricultural landscapes; named regions when captioned |
| Tech | Embedded in the field |
| Colour | Hold true colour; do not LUT everything to neon green or teal-orange |
| Crop | Generous, often 16:9 or 4:5 in cards; full-bleed heroes |
| Overlay | Black ~30% on video heroes for type |

### 11.3 Do use

- Real farmers and field teams (with permission)
- Soil, crop, water infrastructure, residue, drip, standing crop
- Conversations, demonstrations, measurement in situ
- Maharashtra / stated partner geographies
- Stillness and heat; dust; ordinary clothes
- Wide establishing + medium working distance + honest close-ups of hands/soil

### 11.4 Do not use

- `windturbinesinsnow.jpg` and any wind / solar hardware as a climate shorthand
- Generic leaves, globes, green gradients, polar ice
- Corporate handshake, glass-boardroom “impact”
- Cartoon CO₂ molecules, certificate seals, Bitcoin-green
- Staged “smiling farmer + NGO clipboard” stock
- Futuristic AI faces, glowing brains, HUD maps of nowhere
- Unsplash founder substitutes
- Karnataka captions unless the client adds that geography

### 11.5 Image treatment (Brand Rule)

- `object-fit: cover` in rounded frames (`~1.25–2.25rem`) or full-bleed heroes
- Do not put heavy colour overlays (green wash) on photos
- Captions, when used, are factual (place, practice), not inspirational

## 13. Graphic language / shapes

**Observed grammar:**

- **Pills** for buttons, tags, chips
- **Large rounded rectangles** for cards and dark panels (1.25–2.25rem)
- **Circles** for icon wells and loader
- **Hairline rules** (white/10 on dark, `--line` on cream)
- **Uppercase eyebrows** as section keys
- **Giant lowercase wordmark** in the footer band (Sage, huge, cropped) — a signature, use rarely off-site
- **Numbered / stepped process** on services and story timeline (dots + panels). About “who we are” cards were explicitly moved **off** 1-2-3-4 numbering — do not number everything
- **India outline SVG** for geography, not decorative maps of the world
- Grid: CSS/Tailwind columns, not a printed 12-column overlay

**Organic blob shapes, grain textures, and leaf dingbats are not the system.**

## 14. Layout system

### 14.1 Page frame (Brand Rule)

- Max width **1400px**
- Gutters **20 / 32 / 48** (mobile / md / lg)
- Header **65 / 70px**, fixed, transparent then `#132517`
- Sections **64–128px** vertical padding from the existing `py-16`–`py-32` range
- Spacing scale: **4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 20 / 24 / 32 / 48 / 64 / 80 / 96 / 128**

### 14.2 Grid behaviour (Observed)

- 1 column mobile
- 2–3 columns from `md`/`lg` (services, vision, cards)
- Header: logo left, nav right from **1024px**; hamburger below
- Footer: stacked accordion on small screens; multi-column + newsletter from `md`/`lg`; giant mark band underneath

### 14.3 Desktop / tablet / mobile

| | Desktop ≥1024 | Tablet 768–1023 | Mobile |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nav | Inline, 15px, hover lime-green (currently `#2fce65`) | Hamburger | Hamburger, full-height drawer |
| Type | Full clamp() max | Mid | H1 still large; cards stack |
| Photography | Wide cinematic | Same, fewer columns | Full-bleed, taller crops |
| Footer | Grid + mark | Tight grid | Accordion + mark |

### 14.4 Future pages

Start every page with: fixed header, either (a) video/photo hero with veil + H1 or (b) forest/cream editorial hero. Then alternating cream / white / forest bands. End with newsletter + footer. Do not invent a third max-width (1200 or 1440) “to be more standard.”

## 15. UI component system

**Purpose:** Wayfinding on photography.

Leaf on light; lime or sage on dark; underline for active nav. Body links should look like Leaf, not bright blue.

11px, 600, 0.15em, uppercase, Sub or Lime/`#84cc16`. Then H2 sentence case, optional long dek in Sub.

### Cards

| Type | Structure | Notes |
|---|---|---|

Light or forest-embedded. Labels 13–14px. Errors via toast (`#b42318`) and inline. Promise only what operations can keep.

### Footer

Forest, 1400 inner, tagline “Regenerative farming & carbon credits for India’s farmers.” Columns: Company, Solutions, Resources, Legal. Newsletter “Field notes.” Legal bar. Giant lowercase **glaubark**.

**Observed issues (do not copy):** Team/Careers anchors that may not exist; social `href="#"`; some proofing items may already be fixed on Privacy/Terms.

### Process / timeline

About story: dark stage, lime labels, panels, dots, click (not hover-pause) to change. Services: lifecycle of stages as a **product framing** (proofing log: “11-stage” is our numbering — **Client Confirmation Required**).

### Image / video blocks

Hero video + 0.3 black veil. In-content: rounded cover images, gentle 1.03 scale on some hovers.

## 16. Design principles (actionable)

### 1. Technology stays attached to the field

Website: no dashboard-only heroes.  
Presentations: a map or metric beside a photograph, never instead of one.  
Social: phone in a farm > laptop in a café.  
Data viz: axes labelled with crop, district, or practice.

### 2. Lime is a signal, Forest is the room

Do not fill slides with `#a3e635`. Use it for one number, one word, one button.

### 3. Cream is daylight; Forest is after-hours authority

Alternate. A document that is 100% forest feels like a game UI. 100% cream feels like a café menu.

### 4. Type does the prestige; ornaments do not

No chevrons as decoration, no leaf bullets, no watermark globes.

### 5. Claims must be smaller than photography

If the number is unconfirmed, the image and the process carry the page.

### 6. Specific geography over generic climate

A furrow in Maharashtra beats a stock Earth.

### 7. Rounded, not playful

Pills and 20px cards are calm. They are not sticker UI. Do not add emoji, badges with smiles, or bouncing icons.

### 8. Motion is cinematic, not gamified

Lenis/GSAP reveals, clip-path loader, slow image scale. Honour `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## 17. Data and trust visual language

Glaubark’s commercial credibility depends on looking **measurable** without looking **sterile** or **over-claiming**.

### Statistics

- One to three numbers per band, not a dashboard wall.
- Number in DM Sans 600; caption in Inter 14px Sub.
- Colour: white or lime on forest; ink on cream; **never** lime on cream for the number if contrast fails.
- Source the figure in a footnote or “as of [date], [method]” for reports.
- **Brand Rule:** unconfirmed numbers are omitted, not shown as “0%” counters or “xxx”.

### Charts & reports

**Strategic Recommendation** (few charts exist on the marketing site):

- Palette: Forest, Leaf, Lime, Sage, Line, Ink. No rainbow categorical palettes.
- Gridlines: `--line` or white/10.
- No 3D, no gradients in bars, no pie charts for a single part-to-whole if a number will do.
- Annotate in full words (“soil organic carbon, sample plots”) not “SOC!!”.

### Process diagrams

Horizontal or vertical steps, hairline connectors, lime or leaf markers. Prefer 7–11 real process steps from the brief’s cycle rather than a circular “innovation loop.”

### MRV / carbon / verification

Show **method** (satellite pass, soil sample, survey, site visit) as photography + short caption. Do not fake Verra screenshots or certificate PDFs. Status language: support / in development / registered / issued — pick one, accurately.

### Dashboards

None in the repo. If product UI is added later, it should feel like a **field tool** (clear, high contrast, Leaf actions) not a crypto terminal.

## 18. Photography direction (brief)

A longer internal document exists at `photography-guidelines.html` (some geographic facts there are outdated). This section is the brand-level brief.

### Subjects

| Subject | Photograph | Distance | Tone |
|---|---|---|---|
| Farmers | Work and pause, not trophy portraits | Medium; occasional environmental wide | Respect, fatigue, skill |
| Field teams | Conversation, measurement, translation, demo | Medium two-shot | Collegial, not extractive |
| Practices | Drip, residue, compost, rows, tillage absence | Close + context wide | Instructional |
| Soil | Hands, profile, moisture, colour | Close | Tactile, true colour |
| Crops | Standing crop, canopy, intercrop | Wide and detail | Seasonal, local |
| Technology | Device in use outdoors | Over-shoulder, not UI fake | Ordinary |
| Community | Groups that exist; no staged “village meeting” | Wide | Consent first |
| Monitoring | Flags, plots, samples, notebooks | Medium | Procedural |

Lighting: sun and open shade. Avoid on-camera flash. Background: real fields, mills, village edges — not seamless green.

### Do / Don’t

**Do:** ask permission; caption truly; include women as workers not decoration; keep mess (pipes, tarps, bikes).  
**Don’t:** child close-ups for “future generations” campaigns; caste/class stereotyping; drone-only beauty with no people when the story is farmer-first; snow, Alps, California almond stock.

## 20. Future website pages

A new page is Glaubark if it follows all of:

1. **Header + footer** from `js/components.js` (or successor), 1400px inner.
2. **Hero:** full-bleed field video/photo + veil **or** forest/cream editorial; H1 DM Sans; optional 11px eyebrow; no fake stat cluster.
3. **Heading cascade:** one H1, H2 per section, H3 in cards.
4. **CTA:** one lime or leaf pill; secondary ghost.
5. **Rhythm:** 64–128px section padding; alternate white / cream / forest.
6. **Images:** `shoot/` or approved stills; rounded or full-bleed; no green overlay.
7. **Cards:** 1.25rem+ radius, Line or quiet shadow, Leaf icons.
8. **Data:** omit or source.
9. **Density:** editorial (large type, short columns), not Wikipedia and not a landing-page wall of logos.
10. **Mobile:** hamburger <1024px; stack cards; keep H1 brave; accordion footer.
11. **Accessibility:** contrast (Leaf on cream, not Sage); `prefers-reduced-motion`; real labels on inputs; focus rings (forest-mid glow already exists on some controls).
12. **Performance:** compress hero video; do not load unused Swiper/GSAP on legal pages without need; fonts = Inter + DM Sans only.

**Do not** revive `technology.html` as “coming soon” theatre. Ship a page when there is field-true content.
