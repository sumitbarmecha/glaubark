00 — Using this book

The website is the look. The brief is the truth.

This book extracts the implemented Glaubark identity so another professional can extend it. It does not turn placeholder statistics, fictional farmers, or unconfirmed certifications into official brand.

Open the HTML file in a browser to present it. Export to PDF from the browser if needed. Companion files in this folder hold tokens, asset inventory, and content rules. The proofing log at CLIENT-REVIEW-ASSUMED-CONTENT.md remains the list of claims that must not be repeated until Glaubark confirms them.

Do not change production website files from this documentation set.

Classification

Observed Brand rule Strategic recommendation Client confirmation

Observed = in the live site. Brand rule = formalised from that site. Recommendation = future guidance. Confirmation = not a fact until Glaubark says so.

01 — Positioning

Climate finance that starts in the field.

Strategic essence distilled from the live positioning — not a client-supplied slogan. Glaubark is a farmer-first carbon project developer, not an NGO, marketplace, registry, or SaaS product.

Farmers + regenerative agriculture + climate science + carbon finance + technology + measurement.

Every layout and paragraph should make at least two of those legs visible. Never only “green.” Never only “tech.”

Observed on site

What we can stand behind as positioning

  • Farmer-first carbon project developer
  • Bridging regenerative farming and voluntary carbon markets
  • Footer line: regenerative farming and carbon credits for India’s farmers
  • Vision on About: Indian agriculture as a global model for climate action, farmer prosperity, regenerative land management

Do not reuse the home H1 “Offsetting carbon footprint for future generations” on new work — proofed as generic template language.

It should feel

Climate-tech, credible, technical, human, farmer-first, evidence-led, modern, premium, practical.

It should not feel

NGO, charity, generic agribusiness, generic SaaS, crypto, consulting template, stereotypical “green.”

Promise

Farmers who regenerate land should be able to participate in carbon markets through work that is measured and taken seriously — without implying credits are already issued unless that is confirmed.

02 — Personality

Six traits. Each has a failure mode.

Traits are taken from how the site already behaves, not from a generic brand-workshop list.

Brand rule

Grounded

Named geography, Marathwada roots, district-level work. Visual: documentary fields, cream and forest. Avoid rustic pastiche.

Brand rule

Evidence-led

Trust comes from process and measurement language. Avoid shouting unconfirmed percentages — including the live 2,800% / 88.8% hero stats.

Brand rule

Farmer-first

Farmers are project partners. Photograph work, not charity portraits. Never “beneficiaries.”

Brand rule

Technical, not cold

MRV, GIS, baselines — always attached to a field. No dark-mode product theatre.

Brand rule

Considered

Editorial measure, cinematic heroes, lime as a signal. Not luxury fashion; not sticker UI.

Brand rule

Practical

A working cycle: practices, visits, measurement, market access. Manifestos without a mechanism are off-brand.

03 — Story

Keep the sequence. Do not embroider it.

About page structure. Biographical detail should stay aligned with the client — this book does not add new life facts.

  1. Mumbai, Tata Sons. Green-finance research; carbon credits as a way to fund climate-positive projects.
  2. Marathwada. The idea becomes personal: drought, soil, farmer distress as lived knowledge.
  3. Reframe. Credits as farmer finance, not only an accounting instrument.
  4. London. Vatsal Agarwal joins from studies; they found Glaubark Solutions.
  5. Today. Farmers, FPOs, sugar factories, agricultural partners. Maharashtra plus named partner geographies. Hectare totals — confirm

Must stay consistent

Marathwada as root. Research, not a “we always knew” myth. Two founders. Farmer-first company.

Must not be exaggerated

Invented quotes, CEO/COO titles unless confirmed (brief: Founder), Karnataka, “4+ offices,” meet-cute copy.

04 — Voice

Calm, specific, field-literate.

If only a marketer could finish the paragraph, rewrite it. Full rules live in CONTENT_RULES.md.

Do say

  • Farmer-first carbon project developer
  • Regenerative practices on enrolled farms
  • MRV, baseline, verification support
  • Climate finance as rural income
  • District, crop, field team
  • Designed for recognised carbon standards — only if status is accurate

Avoid saying

  • Offsetting carbon for future generations (as the master line)
  • VCS certified / registry-listed (unless confirmed)
  • 2,800% · 88% · agentic AI · SaaS efficiency stats
  • Named fictional farmers (Ramesh, Suresh, draft testimonials)
  • Transform farmland into a carbon asset
  • Crypto, tokens, “saving the planet”

Farmers

Partners, not beneficiaries. Permissioned names only.

Carbon

Projects, credits, voluntary markets. Status verbs must match reality.

Technology

Satellite, GIS, surveys, field tools — not a platform pitch.

Short

Description

Farmer-first carbon projects: regenerative agriculture, measurement, and climate finance in India.

Elevator

Pitch

Glaubark develops carbon projects with farmers and agricultural partners so regenerative work can be measured and connected to voluntary carbon markets — without implying credits are issued until that status is confirmed.

Use together

White or lime on forest. Ink on cream. Leaf on cream for links and icons. Forest text on lime buttons.

Do not use together

Observed --accent and --lime-soft hex values do not match their RGB triplets. Opacity utilities therefore render a paler mint. Align tokens before treating accent as law.

07 — Logo

The live lockup is the white PNG wordmark.

Do not redraw it. Do not quietly replace it with the unused concentric-arc SVG. Clear space and print masters are not specified in the repo — measurements below are recommendations to finalise.

On light

CSS: invert + multiply on light heroes. Prefer a true ink master once supplied.

Heights in product

Header 28px mobile / 60px desktop. Drawer 26px. Footer 30px. Loader mark 68–80px.

Minimum (recommendation)

Do not set the wordmark below 28px digital. Clear space: at least the height of the “g” counter — to be signed off.

Incorrect

Recolour, stretch, drop shadow, leaf dingbats, using logo.svg as official, busy crop behind with no veil.

08 — Imagery

Documentary Indian agriculture. Not climate stock.

Do use

  • Real farmers and field teams, with permission
  • Soil, crop, drip, residue, standing rows
  • Phones and notebooks in the field — technology on the ground
  • Natural light, ordinary clothes, named Indian geographies
  • Library: assets/images/shoot/

Do not use

  • Wind turbines in snow (file exists — anti-pattern)
  • Generic leaves, globes, green gradients, ice caps
  • Boardroom handshakes, cartoon CO₂, crypto seals
  • Staged smiling-farmer stock, Unsplash “founders”
  • Futuristic AI HUDs, Karnataka captions unless added by the client

Hero video: full-bleed, black veil ~30%. No green colour wash on photographs. Icons: stroke SVG, currentColor, round caps — not filled eco packs.

09 — Layout & system

1400px. 20 / 32 / 48 gutters. Forest after cream after white.

Frame

Max width 1400px. Padding 20px / 32px / 48px. Header 65px / 70px, fixed, transparent then #132517.

Space

Scale 4–128. Sections typically 64–128px vertical. Do not invent a 1200px “standard” container.

Shape

Pills 9999px. Cards 1.25–2.25rem. Circles for marks. Hairline rules. Giant lowercase footer word as a rare signature.

Breakpoints

Nav is mobile until 1024px — not 768. Tablet gets padding and columns; desktop gets the inline header. Footer accordion on small screens; giant sage mark band always.

11 — Data & trust

Look measurable. Do not look inflated.

Numbers in DM Sans. Captions in Inter. One to three figures per band. Lime or white on forest; Ink on cream. Source in reports.

Process: hairline steps, not innovation loops. MRV: photograph the method (sample, survey, satellite described in words). No fake Verra screenshots.

Never as brand data

2,800% income · 88.8% input costs · −52 / −93 / −80% SaaS leftovers · ×5 agentic AI · fictional ₹ payments · “VCS certified” as a badge · ∞ partnerships.

12 — Photography brief

Return visits, not a one-day CSR shoot.

Capture

Farmers at work; field teams in conversation; practices; soil; crops; devices outdoors; monitoring plots. Medium two-shots over trophy portraits. Available light. Consent first. No child close-ups as “future generations” props.

Principles that travel

Technology stays attached to the field. Lime is a signal. Cream is daylight; forest is authority. Type does the prestige. Claims stay smaller than photography. Name the place. Motion is cinematic, not gamified.

13 — Applications

One identity, many formats — no “social edition.”

LinkedIn / Instagram

Forest or cream field, one sentence, one shoot photo. No fake-stat carousels. No pastel templates.

Decks & reports

Forest cover + wordmark + one still. Body on cream, ink type, one lime mark. Data from an approved sheet only.

New web pages

Same header/footer, 1400px, Inter + DM Sans, hero with veil or editorial forest/cream, one CTA, no unconfirmed stats. Hamburger below 1024px.

Farmer stories

Only real, permitted people. Location in the first line. Replace the fictional Ramesh Patil article.

Email

Wordmark on forest. Quiet cream body. CTA as a pill, sentence case.

Accessibility

Leaf not sage for small text on cream. Labels on inputs. Honour prefers-reduced-motion.

14 — Before you publish

Consistency checklist

  • Wordmark is the approved PNG/vector, not retyped
  • Palette from tokens — no extra emerald
  • Inter + DM Sans loaded
  • 1400px · 20/32/48 gutters
  • Photography from shoot/ or approved stills
  • Stroke icons, currentColor
  • One primary CTA, sentence case
  • No number without a source
  • No invented testimonials or farmer names
  • Carbon-standard language matches status
  • Farmers described as partners
  • Origin story not exaggerated
  • Draft pages not quoted as live proof
  • Contact, titles, emails confirmed

If a future designer must choose between matching a leftover template number and matching this book, they match this book.
