FAQ

Common questions about working with us

Don’t see yours here? Send us a message and we’ll answer it directly.

Farmers, Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs), sugar factories, corporates with CSR or net-zero targets, NGOs, research institutions, and voluntary carbon market buyers. If you have land, capital, or a goal tied to regenerative agriculture or carbon, we’d love to connect.

Every project starts with a scoping conversation. From there we map land, partners, existing data, and the documentation a carbon project needs — then we share a timeline that matches that context. Farmer onboarding and MRV setup follow once the design is agreed.

Our active operations are currently centred in Maharashtra, but we are expanding into other states. If you’re based elsewhere, still get in touch — we’d like to explore whether a partnership makes sense, especially for larger-scale projects.

Access to verified carbon credits, measurable farmer livelihood impact data, detailed impact reporting, co-branding opportunities, and a clear story of where the climate investment goes. We run the entire programme — from farmer onboarding through MRV to credit issuance.

Scale depends on the crop, the partners, and the methodology. We typically work through FPOs, sugar factories, and farmer groups so a project can support proper MRV. If you are exploring a smaller pilot, tell us the context and we will advise whether it is a fit.
