After an initial scoping call, project design typically takes 4–8 weeks depending on scale and existing data. Farmer onboarding can begin in parallel, with methodology compliance and MRV setup following in subsequent months. We’ll give you a clear timeline for your specific context.

Our active operations are currently centred in Maharashtra, but we are expanding into other states. If you’re based elsewhere, still get in touch — we’d like to explore whether a partnership makes sense, especially for larger-scale projects.

Access to verified carbon credits, measurable farmer livelihood impact data, detailed impact reporting, co-branding opportunities, and a clear story of where the climate investment goes. We run the entire programme — from farmer onboarding through MRV to credit issuance.

For carbon project development, groups of at least 50–100 farmers (roughly 500–1,000 acres) usually make economic sense given MRV and certification costs. That said, we do explore smaller pilots for organisations wanting to test the model before scaling.
