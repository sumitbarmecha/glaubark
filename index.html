How We Work

From farmland to carbon market in four steps

01

Identify & Baseline

Site selection, farmer registration, land assessment, and comprehensive baseline data collection through GIS mapping, digital surveys, and audio-video evidence — the verified foundation for all future monitoring.

Site selection GIS mapping Baseline data
02

Implement & Train

Farmers adopt sustainable interventions — drip irrigation, crop rotation, organic inputs, reduced tillage, and more — with ongoing field guidance, demonstration sessions, and advisory from the Glaubark team.

Practice adoption Field training Input support
03

Monitor & Verify

Satellite imagery, remote sensing, and field evidence are combined into a transparent, audit-ready monitoring record. Independent validators use this to certify project outcomes against the established baseline.

Satellite MRV Third-party audit Reporting
04

Issue & Earn

Verified carbon credits are registered, listed in the voluntary carbon market, and connected to institutional and corporate buyers globally — generating new, climate-linked revenue distributed directly to farmers.

Registry listing Market access Farmer payments
