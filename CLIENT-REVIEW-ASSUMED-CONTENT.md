# Glaubark website — assumed / invented content for client proofing

**Purpose:** The live site was designed before the latest client brief was complete. This document lists every **fact, number, person, quote, certification, contact detail, and story** that we invented, guessed, leftover from a template, or strengthened beyond what the brief said. Use it to mark **Keep / Replace / Remove**.

**How to mark:** write `KEEP`, `REPLACE: …`, or `REMOVE` in the last column (or as comments). Anything not listed here is either taken from the client brief or is generic design copy (headings, CTAs, navigation labels).

**Live pages covered:** Home (`index.html`), About, Founders, Services, Contact, Blog listing, three blog/case articles.  
**Not in the main nav but still in the repo:** `index2.html`, `index3.html` (old homepage drafts with fake testimonials), `process.html`, `technology.html` (placeholder).

**Client brief gaps we did not fill with numbers:** farmer count was written as “xxx thousand”; no named sugar mills, NGOs, validators, logos, or real testimonials were provided. Those are still missing on the site unless noted below.
