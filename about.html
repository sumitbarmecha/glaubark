Who We Are

Built on a farmer-first vision

Glaubark was founded on a simple belief: climate action should not only reduce emissions — it should create real, lasting economic value for the communities most vulnerable to climate change. In India’s drought-prone agricultural heartlands, farmers face compounding pressures: unseasonal rainfall, soil degradation, rising input costs, and volatile crop prices. We exist to turn those challenges into opportunity.

Regenerative farming adoption

We help farmers shift to sustainable practices that restore soil health, cut emissions, and reduce dependency on costly inputs.

Additional income through carbon

Farmers who adopt these practices earn carbon credits, opening a new revenue stream alongside their primary crops.

Climate resilience

Improved soil and water management builds long-term farm resilience against erratic weather patterns and climate stress.

Market access

We connect farmers and partners with voluntary carbon markets and global climate finance, end to end.

Our Story

How Glaubark began

Mumbai
Tata Sons

A research paper that changed direction

Glaubark began with an internship. While working at Tata Sons in Mumbai, co-founder Dhruv Sakore was writing a research paper on green finance and its role in the global energy transition. In the course of that research, he discovered carbon credits — and became deeply interested in their potential to fund climate-positive projects at scale.

Marathwada
Maharashtra

A personal connection to agrarian distress

The idea immediately connected with something personal. Dhruv comes from Marathwada — a drought-prone region of Maharashtra with some of the highest rates of farmer distress in India. Growing up in an agricultural family, he had witnessed firsthand how unseasonal rainfall, depleted soils, volatile crop prices, and limited access to modern farming knowledge erode farmer livelihoods over generations.

The Insight

Carbon credits as a farmer finance tool

This made Dhruv see carbon credits differently — not just as an environmental accounting mechanism, but as a financing tool for farmers. If farmers adopted regenerative practices that improved soil health, reduced emissions, and sequestered carbon, they should also be able to earn from the carbon credit market. Climate action and farmer welfare didn’t have to be separate goals.

London
University

A co-founder joins from London

Dhruv began sharing the idea with friends. One of those conversations reached Vatsal Agarwal, a friend and fellow student in London. Vatsal was looking to build something meaningful and new — and saw strong potential in the vision. After several deep discussions, Vatsal came on board, and together they founded Glaubark Solutions.

Today
Pan-India

From student idea to national presence

What started as a student-led research idea has grown into a company working directly with farmers, FPOs, sugar factories, and agricultural partners across Maharashtra, Punjab, Kerala, and Meghalaya — approaching 100,000 hectares of land under carbon project development.

Why Glaubark Exists

Farmers are on the front lines of climate change. In regions like Marathwada, drought, erratic rainfall, and soil degradation aren’t abstract risks, they’re annual realities that threaten livelihoods.

Regenerative farming practices can change this. They restore soil health, reduce emissions, improve water efficiency, and build long-term resilience. But for small and marginal farmers, accessing carbon markets remains out of reach — too complex, too documentation-heavy, and too disconnected from ground realities.

Glaubark bridges that gap.

We work directly with farmers to adopt better practices, build the monitoring and documentation infrastructure that carbon projects require, and connect verified climate impact to voluntary carbon markets. The result: climate action that generates real, additional income for the people who need it most.

What Makes Glaubark Different

Glaubark doesn’t develop carbon credits on behalf of farmers — it builds carbon projects with them. Farmers are active participants in the process, and direct beneficiaries of the climate finance it generates.

Our field teams live and work in the regions we serve, building trust with farmers through direct, ongoing relationships rather than one-off transactions.

Our founders and senior team spend real time on the ground with farmers and FPOs, not just behind a desk — decisions are shaped by what actually works on-farm.

From farmer onboarding and practice adoption to MRV, verification, and credit issuance, we manage every stage of the carbon project lifecycle in-house.

We design regenerative interventions that fit real farm economics and local conditions, so adoption is practical, not just theoretically sound.

Purpose-built monitoring and data tools give farmers, partners, and carbon buyers a transparent, audit-ready view of impact on the ground.

We treat carbon finance as a means to an end — rural income and resilience — not as a standalone commodity play.

Long-Term Goals

Glaubark’s long-term ambition is to become one of India’s leading carbon project developers — across regenerative agriculture, improved land management, afforestation, agroforestry, and broader land-use based climate solutions. To get there, the company is working toward:

01.Scale that reaches the grassroots

Bringing thousands of small and marginal farmers into the climate finance ecosystem — across geographies, crop types, and farming contexts — through scalable carbon projects built on real field relationships.

02.Credits the market can trust

Developing high-integrity, verifiable carbon credits that meet global buyer expectations, backed by technology-enabled MRV systems that make agricultural carbon projects transparent and audit-ready.

03.Partnerships that multiply impact

Building a network of partners — sugar factories, FPOs, NGOs, CSR teams, corporates, government-linked institutions, and sustainability organizations — that extends Glaubark’s reach and embeds climate action within existing agricultural systems.

04.Better outcomes for farmers

Helping farmers improve soil fertility, reduce input costs, use water more efficiently, and build resilience against climate risk — so the transition to regenerative practice is worth it economically, not just ecologically.

05.A globally credible Indian sustainability company

Positioning Indian agriculture as a meaningful climate solution on the world stage, while building Glaubark into an institution known for rural impact and carbon market integrity — not just credit volume.
